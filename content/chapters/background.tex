\section{Running titles}
The header shows the chapter name and the section name. A name too wide for the header stops the build. Give it a shorter running title with \texttt{\textbackslash chapter[Short title]\{Long title\}} or \texttt{\textbackslash section[Short title]\{Long title\}}. The short title also appears in the contents. The footer shows the short title that \texttt{main.tex} sets with \texttt{\textbackslash shorttitle}.
